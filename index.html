How it works

Your browser times HTTPS requests to every AWS Region’s public DynamoDB endpoint: one warm-up, five samples, median of the middle three. Each round shows one Region’s time. Pick the Region you think it was. Points = 5,000 × e−Δ/40, where Δ is the gap in ms between your pick and the answer.

Light in optical fiber covers about 200 km per millisecond, so a round trip of t ms puts the Region within t / 2 × 200 km of you. Extra delays only make that circle bigger, so the answer is always inside it.

Region list: AWS Systems Manager public parameters. Coordinates: Amazon Location Service geocoding of an approximate city per Region (AWS doesn’t publish exact sites). Map: Amazon Location Service (data sources and attribution). Your location stays in your browser.

Yes, if you open DevTools you can see which Region we’re pinging. Congratulations, you win.

Click a Region, then press Guess

  1. Wait while every Region is measured (a few seconds).
  2. Click the dot of the Region you think matches the time.
  3. Press Guess. You see both Regions’ real times. The closer the times, the more points (up to 5,000). 5 rounds.

At the end you get a map of every Region, colored by how fast it answered you.

Tip: “Set my location” draws the farthest the signal could have gone. The answer is always inside that circle.
